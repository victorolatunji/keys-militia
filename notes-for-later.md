
## Art pass notes (Victor, 2026-11-09; for 9.5 unless noted)
- **Pipeline:** ChatGPT image generation (style ref = standing hero). Character = full-body prompt, then parts-sheet prompt in the same chat; one new chat per character/sheet. Claude cuts sheets into transparent PNGs named by key, never resizing parts individually. Source art lives in assets-src/<folder>/ (not public/) until 9.5. Credits: "generated with ChatGPT (OpenAI), cut and edited by VAO".
- **Character art done:** all 16 in assets-src/<name>/ with reference.png. Humanoids: 10 body parts + jetpack + jet-flame (Robot, Android, Scrap bot: boot flame, no jetpack). Drone: 5 parts. K9: 12 parts incl. separate lower jaw. Folders: player, soldier, mercenary, commando, trooper, zombie, skeleton, infected, robot, android, scrapbot, drone, alien, mutant, cyborg, k9.
- **Rig (9.5):** Phaser Container per character, pivots at neck, shoulder, elbow, hip, knee. Front arm rotates to aim; gun is its own sprite in the gripping hand. Back-side parts setTint(0xbbbbbb). No character has built-in weapons, so any skin can hold any gun.
- **Skins:** every character can be a player skin (cosmetic only), default = blue hero. Needs a clear "this is you" marker. Drone and Zombie K9 are bot only (never skins, no jetpack). Walkers (Zombie, Skeleton, Mutant, Scrap bot) get their jetpack/boot flame only when used as a skin.
- **Machine flight:** Robot, Android, Scrap bot fly on thruster boots (flame under each sole).
- **Customisation:** no character creator. Later, cheap option: skin + colour options (skin tone, hair, outfit) via PNG recolour. Android is white, so it can be tinted freely.
- **Military colours:** keep charcoal/tan; at 9.5 recolour two of the four Military to army green after judging them in-game.
- **Jet Boost visual:** blue flame made by hue-shifting each jet-flame.png (script) + a glow while active.
- **Weapons:** 37 guns in assets-src/weapons/, 7 melee in assets-src/melee/ (knife, machete, bat, spade, hammer, sword, axe), 10 throwables in assets-src/throwables/, 6 utilities in assets-src/utility/. Each gun needs a length and grip point in src/data at 9.5 (Claude can measure grip points). Watch: ZRG 20mm bipod hangs down; axe has a small red mark on the blade (check with gore off).
- **Equipped visuals:** riotShield-held in front of the body, grip in the front hand. armorVest-worn over the torso, scaled per character; on Mutant/Drone/K9 show a blue sheen instead. Health Pack and Jet Boost show nothing when equipped.
- **Airdrops:** the weapon crate is the only crate; it falls as crate-airdrop under the parachute, then the parachute floats off and it swaps to crate-landed. Throwables, utilities and launchers fall on their own (no box, no parachute).
- **Item visibility:** dropped/falling items use their own sprite + colour-coded glow outline by type, idle bob/spin, fall trail, and a name tag when the player is near.
- **Projectiles/effects:** assets-src/projectiles (rocket, grenade-shell, saw-blade, nail, laser-beam, laser-glow, flame-puff) and assets-src/effects (muzzle small/big, explosion 1-4, fire-patch, ice-block at ~70% opacity, dazed-stars, arc-1/2). Tracers, clouds, blood, sparks stay code-drawn.
